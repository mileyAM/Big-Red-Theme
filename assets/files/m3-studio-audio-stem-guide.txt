====================================================================
MILEYM3DIA — STUDIO AUDIO STEM PREP & MASTERING GUIDE
====================================================================

1. STEM EXPORT REQUIREMENTS FOR MIXING
   - Format: 24-Bit / 48kHz or 96kHz WAV (Uncompressed)
   - Headroom: -6dB to -3dB Peak on Master Bus (No Clipper/Limiter)
   - Consolidation: All stems exported from Bar 1, Beat 1 (00:00:00)
   - Naming: [TrackNumber]_[InstrumentName]_[BPM]_[Key].wav

2. VOCAL TRACKING DELIVERABLES
   - Lead Vocal Dry + Reference Print
   - Doubles (L/R) & Harmonies Separated
   - Ad-Lib & FX Tracks Labeled
